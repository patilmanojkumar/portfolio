About Me

I work at the intersection of agricultural economics, statistical modeling, artificial intelligence, and data-driven research systems. My work focuses on transforming agricultural and economic datasets into actionable insights using econometric techniques, machine learning, and computational analytics.

Academic Background

Ph.D. in Agricultural Economics from the University of Agricultural Sciences, Bengaluru. My research interests span price forecasting, risk modeling, market analytics, and AI applications in agricultural systems.

Technical Focus

Experienced in Python, R, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and machine learning workflows. I build analytical systems, dashboards, and AI-powered research tools for applied agricultural and economic problems.

Research Vision

My long-term goal is to bridge data science, AI, and agricultural economics to create scalable analytical systems that support evidence-based policymaking and decision intelligence.
